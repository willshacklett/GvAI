# GV Multi-Agent Survivability — Public Summary

## Result

`gv_recoverable` won the multi-agent survivability test.

## Main idea

Rigid systems preserve too much and adapt poorly.

Reckless systems adapt hard but damage continuity.

Antifragile systems transform well but can still erode coordination.

GV recoverable systems transform while preserving enough truth, constraint, correction, and coherence to remain survivable.

## Core line

> Universal survivability requires recoverable coordination through transformation.
