# GV Battle: Transition vs Magnitude

## Question

Is GV flagging high pressure, or transition into degraded recoverability?

## Why this matters

A detector that flags everything high is just a stress meter.

A useful GV detector should flag transition behavior:

- slope change
- acceleration
- failed normalization
- persistent degradation
- loss of recovery behavior

## Battle win condition

Candidate points should shrink compared to the raw pressure pass.

If candidate windows remain huge, GV is still acting like a magnitude detector.

If candidate windows concentrate around inflection/acceleration periods, GV is moving toward transition detection.
