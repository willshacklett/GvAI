# GV Change Impact Precheck

## Purpose

The GV Change Impact Precheck scores whether an organization can safely absorb a proposed change.

The goal is not to decide whether a change sounds good.  
The goal is to decide whether the system remains recoverable after the change is introduced.

## Core Principle

Every new change consumes organizational recovery capacity.

Middle management often carries the real operational burden of change:
- communication
- training
- troubleshooting
- morale management
- reporting
- frontline resistance
- performance recovery

## GV Modes

### ALLOW

The change is clear, paced, trained, and supported.

### QUALIFY

The change may proceed only if leadership adds support, reduces burden, improves communication, or slows the rollout.

### BLOCK

The change should not roll out yet because it risks manager overload, operational damage, or loss of recoverability.

## Risk Inputs

- manager_overload
- training_gap
- communication_gap
- recovery_gap
- stacking_risk

## Corporate Translation

Do not add organizational change without protecting the people responsible for absorbing it.
