# Human Servers vs Recoverability

Modern civilization increasingly treats humans as infrastructure.

Always available.
Always connected.
Always responsive.
Always producing.

Humans become biological servers.

This creates a hidden recoverability problem.

## Gv framing

A system can remain capable while becoming unrecoverable.

For humans this appears as:

- burnout
- exhaustion
- emotional flattening
- degraded long-horizon reasoning
- shortened planning horizons
- dependency on external stabilization systems
- collapse after prolonged continuity debt

The failure is often invisible until recovery becomes impossible.

## Server mode

Servers are expected to:

- maximize uptime
- minimize interruption
- tolerate continuous demand
- recover instantly
- suppress internal instability

This works for machines.

It does not work for biological intelligence.

## Gv interpretation

Human intelligence is substrate-bound intelligence.

The substrate requires:

- sleep
- nutrition
- safety
- social stability
- psychological continuity
- recovery cycles
- time away from active demand

Without recoverability, continuity eventually collapses.

## Core principle

Humans are not failing because they are weak.

They are failing because they are being operated like infrastructure.

## Final compression

Recovery is not separate from intelligence.

Recovery is part of intelligence.
