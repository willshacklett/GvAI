# GV TOE Foundation

## Position

The long-term target is TOE-level:

GV is being explored as a foundational continuity condition for persistent existence.

## Core intuition

Anything that persists must preserve continuity through change.

That continuity may appear as:

- recoverability
- persistence
- directional coherence
- resistance to irrecoverable degradation
- relational order surviving transformation

## Important distinction

GV is not being reduced to:

- a tuned scalar
- PCA
- a machine-learning projection
- a convenience metric

Those are downstream tools.

The foundation is:

Persistence requires continuity.

## TOE-level hypothesis

If GV is real at TOE scope, then every persistent system must express some continuity-preserving structure.

That structure may not always be scalar.

It may appear as:

- order
- topology
- invariance
- causal structure
- recoverability geometry
- scale-coupled persistence

## Required humility

This does not declare TOE proven.

It defines what TOE-level GV must survive.

## TOE falsification pressure

GV weakens if persistent systems exist with:

- no recoverability structure
- no continuity-preserving relation
- no invariant ordering
- no causal persistence constraint
- no distinction between reversible disturbance and irreversible degradation

## TOE strengthening pressure

GV strengthens if many domains independently show:

- continuity order survives harmless transformation
- irreversible degradation breaks continuity
- local/global decoupling is measurable
- scalar failures reveal richer continuity structure instead of randomness
- persistence depends on recoverability constraints

## Research rule

Do not shrink the target because tests are hard.

Do not protect the target by hiding failures.

TOE remains the horizon.

Evidence decides the path.
