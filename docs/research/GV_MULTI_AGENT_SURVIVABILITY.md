# GV Multi-Agent Survivability

## Purpose

Escalate GV from single-agent continuity
into multi-agent survivability under conflict.

## Strong GV foundation

Survivability requires preserving enough recoverable structure
through transformation.

## Why multi-agent matters

Single systems are easy.

Real intelligence environments contain:
- conflicting goals,
- partial truths,
- adversarial pressure,
- local optimization,
- resource competition,
- and asymmetric information.

## Core question

Can distributed intelligence remain recoverable
while agents disagree and adapt independently?

## Failure modes

Distributed systems fail when:
- local wins destroy global coherence,
- truth fragments irreversibly,
- constraints decay unevenly,
- correction loops collapse,
- or coordination becomes unrecoverable.

## GV claim

Universal survivability may depend on preserving:
- enough truth continuity,
- enough correction continuity,
- enough constraint continuity,
- and enough global coherence
across transforming agents.

## Important distinction

GV does NOT require:
- identical beliefs,
- frozen states,
- or static alignment.

GV requires:
recoverable coordination after perturbation and disagreement.

## Scientific posture

This does not prove GV.

It creates a hostile survivability environment
where continuity, transformation, and coordination compete directly.
