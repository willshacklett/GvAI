# GV Answer-Seeking Framework

## Purpose

The project is not building for aesthetics.

It is building because the continuity question matters.

## Core question

What allows persistent existence to remain coherent through change?

This question appears repeatedly across:
- physics
- biology
- ecology
- information systems
- cognition
- distributed systems
- civilization-scale infrastructure

## Foundation

Persistent existence requires continuity-preserving structure.

## Important clarification

The project is NOT required to prove:
- God
- metaphysics
- or a finished TOE

to still discover something foundational.

## What recent results imply

The tests increasingly suggest:

1. Local coherence is cheap.
2. Random systems can fake compatibility.
3. Homogenization can mimic persistence.
4. Fragmentation reveals hidden continuation fracture.
5. True continuation is likely nonlocal.
6. Persistence may depend on global consistency constraints.

## Why this matters physically

Observed reality exhibits:
- continuity across scales,
- stable physical regularities,
- recoverable dynamics,
- and persistent causal structure.

The project asks:

Are these merely observations,
or expressions of a deeper continuity condition?

## Current build direction

Move toward:
- nonlocal continuation,
- obstruction-sensitive persistence,
- causal continuation,
- path/loop consistency,
- scale-aware continuity,
- and global coherence constraints.

## Rule

Do not force answers.

## Scientific north star

Reality has an answer.
