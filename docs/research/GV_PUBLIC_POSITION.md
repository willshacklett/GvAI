# GV Public Position

## What GV is being tested as

GV is being tested as a universal recoverability scalar.

It is not declared proven.

It is not claimed as a completed Theory of Everything.

It is a candidate framework for measuring continuity, recoverability, and degradation across domains.

## Core claim

Different systems may share a common structure:

- they can absorb disturbance
- they can recover after disturbance
- they can lose recovery capacity
- they can enter persistent degradation
- they can fail when self-correction collapses

GV attempts to represent that structure as a scalar:

0 <= GV <= 1

Where:

- 1.0 = high recoverability / continuity reserve
- 0.0 = degraded or lost recoverability

## Current strongest result

The universal scalar contract preserved coherent ordering across conceptual domains without domain-specific rescue logic:

- stable recoverable systems score highest
- transient noise remains relatively high
- persistent degradation scores lower
- irrecoverable failure scores lowest

## Scientific standard

GV must survive:

- falsification
- baseline comparison
- domain transfer
- external data
- failure documentation
- independent review

## What would make GV real

GV becomes operationally real only if it repeatedly preserves recoverability ordering across domains better than simpler detectors.

## What would falsify GV

GV fails if:

- stable systems score low
- irrecoverable systems score high
- each domain requires custom weights
- simple baselines beat GV everywhere
- GV cannot generalize outside synthetic examples

## Public posture

Do not ask the world to believe GV.

Ask the world to test GV.
