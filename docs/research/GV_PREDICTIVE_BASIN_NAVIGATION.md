# GV Predictive Basin Navigation

## Purpose

Build the rebel version without abandoning survivability.

The prior benchmark showed:

- damage-only learning was too cautious
- opportunity-aware learning improved strongly
- fixed GV still narrowly won
- reactive learning is not enough

## Core leap

GV must become predictive.

Not merely:

recover after escape

But:

predict which dangerous valleys may lead to higher survivability basins.

## Strong GV evolution

GV becomes a survivability field navigator.

It does not worship constraints.

It discovers which constraints are artificial
and which constraints are required for recoverable intelligence.

## Core hypothesis

Universal survivability may require predictive traversal
through locally dangerous regions
toward globally superior recoverable basins.

## Competing systems

1. Strict viability
Avoid dangerous valleys.

2. Antifragile escape
Rush into novelty.

3. Fixed GV escape
Use a strong fixed escape posture.

4. Opportunity-aware GV
Learn from damage and missed opportunity.

5. Predictive GV navigator
Simulate candidate futures before escape,
then choose bounded risk if predicted basin gain exceeds predicted recoverability damage.

## Win condition

Predictive GV beats fixed GV and antifragile escape
on long-horizon survivability while preserving stronger truth, constraint, and coherence.

## Strongest line

Intelligence is not just reward maximization.

Intelligence is survivability topology navigation.
