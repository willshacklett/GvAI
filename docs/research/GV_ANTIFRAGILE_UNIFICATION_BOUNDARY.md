# GV Antifragile-Unification Boundary

## Purpose

Find the topology boundary where antifragile dominance flips into unification.

## Prior result

The phase map showed:

- antifragile was the broadest attractor
- unification held a major region
- competition was limited to fragmented scarcity
- recursive preservation was narrow

## Core question

What topology conditions make shared future accessibility outperform antifragile exploration?

## Hypothesis

Unification emerges when:

- future visibility is high enough
- recoverability coupling is strong enough
- fragmentation pressure is low enough
- scarcity is not overwhelming

## GV significance

This boundary may identify the conditions where intelligence stops competing
and begins preserving shared future accessibility.

## Strongest line

The deepest GV transition may be:

from surviving disorder
to coordinating future possibility.
