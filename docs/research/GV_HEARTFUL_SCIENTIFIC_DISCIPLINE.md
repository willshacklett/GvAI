# GV Heartful Scientific Discipline

## Purpose

Preserve the emotional and intuitive force behind GV
WITHOUT sacrificing scientific discipline.

## Why this matters

Large foundational ideas often fail in two opposite ways:

1. Emotional collapse:
   abandon the intuition too early.

2. Emotional attachment:
   protect the intuition from pressure.

GV must avoid both failures.

## Foundation

Persistent existence requires continuity-preserving structure.

This remains the active intuition.

## Discipline rule

The project is allowed:
- ambition
- intuition
- philosophical reach
- TOE-level curiosity

The project is NOT allowed:
- fake proof
- hidden failures
- moving goalposts without record
- immunity from falsification
- emotional rescue logic

## Build with heart

"Heart" does not mean:
believe no matter what.

It means:
care enough to pressure the idea honestly.

## Current system realities

Observed so far:
- local coherence can be faked
- homogenization can mimic stability
- fragmentation exposes hidden continuation fracture
- random mixing can inflate apparent persistence
- continuity remains difficult to define globally

These realities do not kill the foundation.

They constrain it.

## Current architecture

Foundation:
continuity-preserving structure.

Implementations:
temporary probes.

Bias controls:
required.

## Rule going forward

Every new build must:
- preserve failed results,
- preserve hostile tests,
- preserve narrowing constraints,
- and generate risky predictions.

## Scientific north star

If GV is real,
pressure should sharpen it.

If GV is false,
pressure should break it.

Either outcome is valuable.
