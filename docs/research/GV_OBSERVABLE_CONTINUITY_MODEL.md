# GV Observable Continuity Model

## Core idea

We cannot directly observe every microscopic or hidden state.

But stable observable behavior can constrain what hidden structure must be doing.

## Principle

Observable continuity constrains invisible structure.

If a system repeatedly returns, stabilizes, persists, or degrades in measurable ways,
then the hidden layer is not arbitrary.

It is expressing recoverability structure.

## GV framing

GV does not require direct access to the smallest layer.

GV estimates whether observable macro-behavior implies:

- strong recovery
- weakening recovery
- persistent degradation
- local/global decoupling
- irreversible loss

## Micro-to-macro statement

Small interactions may be hidden, noisy, or unobservable.

But their aggregate effects can become stable, measurable, and engineerable.

GV attempts to measure the continuity signal that survives that aggregation.

## Scientific claim

GV is not a microscope.

GV is a continuity inference model.

It asks:

Given only observable behavior, how much recoverability must the hidden system still possess?

## Falsification

The model fails if observable recovery patterns do not constrain hidden recoverability better than simpler baselines.

The model strengthens if observable GV consistently tracks hidden degradation in simulations where the hidden state is known.
