# GV Right or Wrong Framework

## Purpose

Preserve the correct scientific posture for a foundational search.

## Core position

GV may be:
- right,
- partially right,
- structurally incomplete,
- or completely wrong.

The project must remain capable of discovering all four outcomes.

## Foundation

Persistent existence requires continuity-preserving structure.

This remains the active hypothesis.

## Why the project continues

Not because GV is proven.

Because the continuity question continues generating:
- real distinctions,
- real constraints,
- real system realities,
- and deeper unresolved structure.

## Important rule

Do not confuse:
- emotional conviction
with
- scientific proof.

But also do not confuse:
- approximation failure
with
- immediate foundation collapse.

## Current realities

Observed:
- local coherence can be faked
- random mixing can mimic stability
- fragmentation reveals hidden continuation fracture
- nonlocal continuation remains unresolved
- bias risk is active
- continuity still appears physically relevant

## Scientific discipline

Every build must:
- preserve failed results,
- preserve hostile tests,
- preserve narrowing constraints,
- preserve bias audits,
- and generate risky predictions.

## Why this matters

If continuity is foundational,
the answer could matter for:
- intelligence,
- safety,
- recoverability,
- biology,
- infrastructure,
- physics,
- and long-term survivability.

If continuity is NOT foundational,
the project should eventually fail honestly.

## North star

Build honestly enough that reality can answer:
right or wrong.
