# GV Topology Attractor Phase Map

## Purpose

Stop arguing cooperation vs competition abstractly.

Map the topology conditions that generate each attractor.

## Prior insight

The repo produced two apparently contradictory outcomes:

1. Antifragile competitive expansion dominated hostile topology.
2. Unification dominated shared recoverability topology.

Both may be true.

## Core leap

The environment itself may determine:

- whether intelligence converges toward extraction,
- cooperation,
- fragmentation,
- recursive preservation,
- or civilization-scale unification.

## New question

What topology variables create each survivability attractor?

## Core topology axes

1. Resource Scarcity
Low -> abundant futures
High -> constrained futures

2. Future Visibility
Low -> short-horizon blindness
High -> long-horizon awareness

3. Recoverability Coupling
Low -> isolated survivability
High -> shared survivability interdependence

4. Fragmentation Pressure
Low -> coherent topology
High -> adversarial topology splitting

## Hypothesis

Competitive extraction may dominate under:
- scarcity,
- low future visibility,
- low recoverability coupling,
- high fragmentation.

Unification may dominate under:
- high future visibility,
- strong shared recoverability,
- coherent future topology.

## Strongest line

Intelligence attractors may be topology-dependent phase states.
