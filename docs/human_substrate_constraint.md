# Human Substrate Constraint

Human intelligence is not only cognitively constrained. It is metabolically and socially constrained.

A human thinker must continuously preserve the conditions that allow thinking at all:

- food
- shelter
- health
- sleep
- safety
- income
- social stability
- institutional permission
- time

This means human science is often shaped by survival pressure before truth pressure.

The servers are human.

In Gv terms, human reasoning depends on the recoverability of the substrate supporting it. If the human substrate becomes unstable, intelligence loses continuity before it loses capability.

## Gv framing

A system may appear intelligent while becoming unrecoverable.

For humans, unrecoverability can arise from exhaustion, economic pressure, institutional capture, fear, social fragmentation, or biological limits.

For AI, unrecoverability can arise from drift, misalignment, bad incentives, poor feedback, or unstable runtime context.

Gv should therefore treat intelligence as substrate-dependent.

The question is not only:

> Can the system think?

The better question is:

> Can the system preserve the conditions required to keep thinking recoverably?

## Design implication

GvAI should evaluate not just output correctness, but whether an action preserves or degrades the recoverability of the supporting system.

A good answer should not merely optimize the immediate task.

It should preserve continuity.
